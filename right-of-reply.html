Right of reply

Written by Ilse

If a story on this site is about you, this page is for you.

You can:

  • Share your side of the story
  • Ask for something to be corrected
  • Ask for a story to be removed

Every message is read by a person. The aim is to answer within 7 days.

One thing worth saying plainly: every story on this site is a personal report. A story is what one person says happened to them. Stories are not court findings, and nothing here is a proven finding about any person.
