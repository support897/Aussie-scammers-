Names and profiles: Valeria Hackett and Joshua Hackett

Posted 9 October 2026 · Updated 11 October 2026

The couple in these stories have used more than one name, and more than one profile. This page lists them all in one place, with the photos people shared, so you can check before you trust a name.

The names

  • Valeria Hackett: the name on the Facebook profile and the Messenger chats in the screenshots shared with the stories
  • Valeria Hacket: the spelling used most in the 30 June 2025 post
  • Valeria Hojem: the name in the rental report, Story 5, and in other stories
  • Valery Hojem: the name on the RightPaw profile used in Story 6
  • Valeria Ramos: the full form of the Val Ramos name, shared with this page
  • Val Ramos: the name on the Facebook profile shared with this page in October 2026
  • Zaira Gallardo: named together with Valeria Hojem in the Story 5 report of 1 July 2023
  • Joshua Hacket: Valeria's husband, named in Story 1. Also written Joshua J. Hackett

The QCAT decision of 28 January 2026 names the respondents as Joshua J. Hackett and Valeria Hackett (case Q10777-25). The paper is on Story 1.

How they look: beware

These photos were shared with this page. They show the two people named in the stories. Names and profiles can change. Faces are harder to change. Check the face before you start work, book a stay, rent out a home, or hand over anything of yours.

Every photo on this page is them. Only them. Learn their faces.
