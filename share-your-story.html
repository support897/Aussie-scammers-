Were you scammed too? Share your story

Written by Ilse

Your story can protect the next person. Keep it simple. Say what happened, in your own words.

How this works: your story does not go live on its own. A person reads every story first. They take out private details, check the words, and only then does a story go up. Nothing is posted automatically. You can choose to stay anonymous.

Stories shared here are personal reports. A story is what one person says happened to them. Stories are not court findings.

Please cover phone numbers, addresses, and bank details in your images before you add them.

This form is not live yet. Sending is switched off until the site is connected, so nothing you type here is sent or saved. This note will be removed when the form goes live.

Too hard to write it all?

Short is fine. Three or four honest sentences help more than silence.

You can also report a scam to Scamwatch at any time.
