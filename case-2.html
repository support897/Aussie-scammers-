Story 2 — The Airbnb stay, as reported by a guest

Reported by: an anonymous guest. Their comment was shared in the screenshots attached to the post behind Story 1. This page is a short summary of that report, in our words.

Please read this first: This story is a personal report. It is what one person says happened to them. It is not a court finding, and nothing here is a proven finding about any person.

What this guest says happened

The guest says they had a similar situation, and that they stayed at the Airbnb run by the couple in Story 1.

They say the booking was cancelled only hours before their arrival, and that they felt they had no other choice at that point.

They describe Joshua as threatening towards them, and say it made them feel very unsettled — in the shared screenshot, their sentence is cut off partway through that thought, so this page does not guess at the missing words.

They say that, from their experience, the couple were not great people to deal with. They say they cut their stay short, and that they lost money.

In the same screenshot, the author of Story 1 replies to this guest, thanks them for speaking up, says she is sorry they went through it, and asks if she can message them.

What this page does not show

The shared screenshot starts mid-sentence. It does not show this guest's name, the date of the stay, or the amount they say they lost. So this page shows none of those things.
