Cancelled hours before arrival — a guest's Airbnb stay

Posted 9 October 2026 · Story told by an Airbnb guest, who stays anonymous

A guest wrote this under that Facebook post, in a comment shared with it. It is written out here in short.

What happened

The guest says they had a similar situation, and that they stayed at the Airbnb run by the couple in Story 1.

Hours before they were due to arrive, the booking was cancelled on them. They say they felt they had no other choice at that point.

They describe Joshua as threatening towards them, and say it made them feel "super" — that is where their sentence is cut off in the screenshot that was shared, so the missing word is not guessed here.

They say the couple were, in their experience, not great people to deal with. They cut their stay short, and they lost money.

In the same screenshot, Ilse replied to this guest, thanked them for speaking up, and asked if she could message them.
