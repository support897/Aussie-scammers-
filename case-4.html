Cancelled hours before arrival: a guest's Airbnb stay

Posted 9 October 2026 · Told by an Airbnb guest, who stays anonymous

A guest wrote this in a comment under the Facebook post, in the Surfers Paradise community. The full comment is shown below.

What happened

The guest stayed at the Airbnb run by the couple in Story 1, arriving in April. A few hours before arrival, the booking was cancelled on them, and they felt they had no other choice.

In their words, Joshua was "pretty threatening" towards them, and made them feel "super uncomfortable". In their experience, the couple are not great people to deal with.

They cut their stay short, and lost a lot of money.

Ilse replied to the comment, thanked them for speaking up, and asked if she could message them.

The details
  • When: arriving in April. The comment does not give the year.
  • Where: not shared.
  • In short: a booking cancelled hours before arrival. Joshua described as pretty threatening, and the guest made to feel super uncomfortable. The stay was cut short, and a lot of money was lost.
  • What they lost: a lot of money, in their words. The amount is not given.
  • Who they told: they spoke up in the comments under the post.
