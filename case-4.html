A puppy, a payment that failed — Susan's story

Posted 9 October 2026 · Story told by Susan Kim

Susan wrote to me in September 2026. On 24 September she told me she was welcome for her situation to be told, so her name is on this story. — Ilse

What happened

Susan says she has been a registered dog breeder for 27 years, and that nothing like this had ever happened to her before.

At the start of the week of Monday 14 September 2026, a couple came to collect a puppy from her. She says they came across as a lovely couple with a baby, and nothing gave her a reason to feel suspicious.

The payment was made through RightPaw. Susan let the puppy go before the payment had cleared. She says she has done that many times before, with no problems.

On the Wednesday, 16 September, an email told her the payment had failed.

After that, she says, her calls and messages were not answered.

She reported the puppy as stolen through the microchip company.

On the Monday, 21 September, she went to the police. She says the officer told her the police did not see it as a police matter, because an agreement had been made — that it belonged in the civil courts, with QCAT mentioned — and told her to call Policelink.

The details

  • When: the puppy was collected at the start of the week of 14 September 2026; the payment-failed email arrived on Wednesday 16 September 2026.
  • Where: Not shared.
  • In short: a puppy collected before a RightPaw payment had cleared; the payment then failed; calls and messages after that went unanswered, as Susan reports it.
  • Reported to: the microchip company, which she reported the puppy to as stolen; and the police on 21 September 2026, who she says called it a civil / QCAT matter and told her to call Policelink.

Susan shared photos and contact details with me in her private messages. Private details are never published on this site.
