Story 4 — The puppy and the payment that failed, as reported

Reported by: Susan Kim, in messages in September 2026. She said her situation is welcome to be told. This page is a short summary of her report, in our words.

Please read this first: This story is a personal report. It is what one person says happened to her. It is not a court finding, and nothing here is a proven finding about any person.

What she says happened

She says she has been a registered dog breeder for 27 years, and that nothing like this had ever happened to her before.

She says a couple collected a puppy from her at the start of the week of 14 September 2026. She says they came across as a lovely couple with a baby, and nothing gave her a reason to feel suspicious.

She says the payment was made through RightPaw, and she let the puppy go before the payment had cleared. She says she had done that many times before with no problems.

On the Wednesday, she says, an email told her the payment had failed.

After that, she says, her calls and messages were not answered.

She says she reported the puppy as stolen through the microchip company.

She says she went to police the following Monday. In her account, the officer said police did not see it as a police matter because an agreement had been made, that it belonged in the civil courts, and told her to call Policelink.

She says she was still deciding whether she had the strength to take it further, because she does not want them to get away with it, and also does not need more stress.

What is not shown here

She shared photos and contact details in her private messages. Private details are never published on this site.
