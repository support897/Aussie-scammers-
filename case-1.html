The pay that stopped — my Airbnb and baby-care work

Posted 9 October 2026 · Story told by Ilse

My story

I moved to the Gold Coast about three months before I wrote my Facebook post on 30 June 2025. I had worked as a nanny in Brisbane and in Vancouver, and I was hoping to keep doing that work here.

Valeria asked me to care for the couple's newborn once the family came back from the United States. While I waited for that to start, I took a job managing their Airbnb, working for Valeria and her husband Joshua.

Then my pay stopped.

That was about a month and a half before my post. The reasons I was given were bank problems, and that I would be paid when the family was back in Australia. I stayed patient. I kept working.

When the family came back, the baby-care job was cancelled with no warning — the baby was put in daycare instead. And the pay for the month and a half of work I had already done was refused.

My boyfriend and I went to talk to them in person. We were told they were not happy with my work. That is the part that still hurts, because I hold messages from that time praising my work, day after day.

The messages I shared with my post talk about pay being pending while a bank account was verified, about the family being out of Australia for months and the trip back, about an offer to sort the pay the next week, and about Joshua being able to pay, including paying in cash.

I reported it to Fair Work. At the time of my post, I was looking for a lawyer.

The details

  • When: the work started about three months before 30 June 2025; the pay stopped about a month and a half before that date.
  • Where: Gold Coast, Queensland.
  • In short: Airbnb management work for Valeria and Joshua, with baby-care work promised after their return; the pay stopped, the baby-care work was cancelled, and pay for the work already done was refused.
  • Reported to: Fair Work, as I said in my post.

Update

In September 2026, in my own messages, I said I had been to QCAT about this and won, and that I had also reported it to police, with nothing happening yet from that. I want to be clear about the wording: Ilse says she won her QCAT case — that is my own statement, and the QCAT document itself is not on this site.
