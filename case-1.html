Story 1 — The work that was not paid, as reported

Reported by: the worker herself, in a public Facebook post dated 30 June 2025. This page is a short summary of her report, in our words.

Please read this first: This story is a personal report. It is what one person says happened to her. It is not a court finding, and nothing here is a proven finding about any person.

What she says happened

She says she had recently moved to the Gold Coast, hoping to keep working as a nanny, work she had done with care in Brisbane and Vancouver.

She says Valeria contacted her about caring for a newborn once the family returned from the United States. While she waited, she says she was offered work helping to manage an Airbnb run by Valeria and her husband, Joshua.

She says she accepted because she wanted to support someone from her own country, and turned down other work to stay loyal to them.

Then, she says, her pay stopped — about a month and a half before she wrote her post.

She reports that the reasons she was given were about bank problems, and that she would be paid when the family was back in Australia. She says she stayed patient and kept working.

After the family returned, she says the promised newborn care work was cancelled without warning, with the baby placed in daycare instead.

She also says payment for the month and a half of work she had already done was refused. She says that when she and her partner went to speak with them in person, they were told the family was not happy with her work. She says messages she holds praise her work day by day, which is part of why this hurt so much.

She says she reported the matter to Fair Work and was looking for a lawyer.

In a later message, in September 2026, she says she went to QCAT about the couple and won, and that she also reported them to police. That is her own statement in her messages. The QCAT document itself has not been shown on this site.

Evidence mentioned in her report

Her post included screenshots of messages. In summary, the messages she shared touch on:

  • Pay being described as pending while a bank account was verified
  • Being outside Australia for some months, and travel back being planned
  • An offer to sort out payment the following week, including payment in cash, and Joshua being able to pay

Screenshots are described here, not shown in full, so private details stay private.
