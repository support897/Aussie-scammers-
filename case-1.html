The pay that stopped — Ilse's Airbnb and baby-care work

Posted 9 October 2026 · Story 1 — Ilse's story

Her story

This is Ilse's story. She lives on the Gold Coast. She moved there about three months before she wrote her Facebook post on 30 June 2025. She had worked as a nanny in Brisbane and in Vancouver, and she was hoping to keep doing that work in her new home.

Valeria asked her to care for the couple's newborn once the family came back from the United States. While she waited for that to start, Ilse took a job managing their Airbnb, working for Valeria and her husband Joshua.

Then her pay stopped.

That was about a month and a half before her post. The reasons she says she was given were bank problems, and that she would be paid when the family was back in Australia. She stayed patient. She kept working.

When the family came back, the baby-care job was cancelled with no warning — the baby was put in daycare instead. And the pay for the month and a half of work she had already done was refused.

Ilse and her boyfriend went to talk to them in person. They were told the couple were not happy with her work. She says that is the part that still hurts, because she holds messages from that time praising her work, day after day.

The messages she shared with her post talk about pay being pending while a bank account was verified, about the family being out of Australia for months and the trip back, about an offer to sort the pay the next week, and about Joshua being able to pay, including paying in cash.

She says she reported it to Fair Work. At the time of her post, she was looking for a lawyer.

The details
  • When: the work started about three months before 30 June 2025; the pay stopped about a month and a half before that date.
  • Where: Gold Coast, Queensland.
  • In short: Airbnb management work for Valeria and Joshua, with baby-care work promised after their return; the pay stopped, the baby-care work was cancelled, and pay for the work already done was refused, as Ilse reports it.
  • Reported to: Fair Work, as Ilse said in her post.

Update

Ilse says she later went to QCAT and won, and reported the matter to police. That is her statement, made in her own messages in September 2026, where she also said nothing had happened yet from the police report. This blog has not seen the QCAT paper, or any document from it.

If unpaid work sounds familiar, Fair Work can explain your options. You can also report a scam to Scamwatch, or share your story here — stories are checked by a person before anything goes live, and you can stay anonymous.
