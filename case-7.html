The deposit that never came back — an Airbnb guest's story

Posted 11 October 2026 · Shared by a man who asked to stay anonymous

This story comes from a message sent on 18 October 2025 to the woman in Story 1, after her warning post. His message is shown below, with the names covered. What his case is about was described to this blog by her.

What happened

In his message he wrote that he was "from the AirBnB from Joshua and Valeria Hackett" — a guest of the Airbnb run by the couple named on this blog.

His case, as described to this blog, is about his Airbnb deposit: it was not paid back to him.

He wrote that he also had problems with them, and that he had already had a hearing at GC Court — the Gold Coast court.

He asked if the woman in Story 1 had any information about them, or if they were still at GC — on the Gold Coast.

The details
  • When: his message is dated 18 October 2025. The hearing he mentions had already happened by then.
  • Where: the Gold Coast, as he wrote ("GC").
  • In short: an Airbnb deposit that was not paid back, as his case was described to this blog; he wrote that he also had problems with the couple and had already had a hearing at GC Court.
  • Reported to: he wrote that he had already had a hearing at GC Court.

This page cannot show his booking, any deposit record, or any court paper — none of that was shared with this blog. The amount of the deposit is not known to this blog, so it is not listed here.
