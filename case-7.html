The deposit that never came back: an Airbnb guest's story

Posted 11 October 2026 · Shared by a man who stayed at the couple's Airbnb, who stays anonymous

He wrote to Ilse on 18 October 2025, after her warning post. His message is shown below, with his name and hers covered.

What happened

In his message, he introduces himself as a guest from the Airbnb of Joshua and Valeria Hackett. He writes that he also had problems with them, and that he had already had a hearing at GC Court, on the Gold Coast.

He asked if Ilse had any information about them, and if they are still on the Gold Coast.

As his case was described to this page, the couple never paid his Airbnb deposit back, and his hearing was at QCAT.

The details
  • When: he wrote on 18 October 2025. The stay itself is not dated in his message.
  • Where: Gold Coast, Queensland.
  • In short: an Airbnb guest whose deposit was never paid back, and who had already had a court hearing about the couple.
  • What he lost: his Airbnb deposit. The amount is not given.
  • Who he told: he took it to court himself, and he wrote to Ilse after her post.
