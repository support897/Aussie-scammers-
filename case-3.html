The rent, the receipt and the locksmith day — an apartment report

Posted 9 October 2026 · Shared by a person who asked to stay anonymous

This report was first posted on 1 July 2023, in a Facebook group for Mexicans in Australia. It was written in Spanish. This page is a short English summary of it, and the original post itself is shown below.

What happened

The report names two people, exactly as written in it: "Valeria Hojem" and "Zaira Gallardo". The person who reported it says they rented their apartment to them.

From the beginning, the reporter says, paying the bond and the first two weeks of rent was "a game". They were sent a receipt for a transfer from Mexico. They say the money from that transfer never arrived, and that the receipt was false.

They say the pair then arrived and paid two weeks of rent — and after that, disappeared. Texts and calls were never answered again.

The reporter says they had to pay a locksmith to open the apartment door. That morning, they say, the pair answered from another number, and Valeria said she would come to the apartment between 2pm and 3pm. The reporter says she did not come.

The reporter says they were blocked, so they could not tag the pair in the post. The report was shared to warn others in the Mexican community, so more people would not be left in the same position.

The details
  • When: the report was posted on 1 July 2023. The dates of the rental itself are not stated in it.
  • Where: Not shared.
  • In short: a transfer receipt from Mexico that the reporter says was false, with the money never arriving; two weeks of rent then paid, then disappearance; contact blocked; the reporter paid a locksmith; a promised 2–3pm arrival that did not happen.
  • Reported to: Not shared.
