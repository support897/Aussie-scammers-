Story 3 — The apartment rent, as reported

Reported in a comment screenshot headed Karla Trujillo, shared with the post behind Story 1. The original report was written in Spanish. This page is a short summary of it in English, in our words.

Please read this first: This story is a personal report. It is what one person says happened to them. It is not a court finding, and nothing here is a proven finding about any person.

What this person says happened

This person reports Valeria, under the names Valeria Hojem and Valeria Gallardo, over a rented apartment.

She says the agreed rent was not paid. She says she was then blocked, so she could no longer reach them.

She says that at the start she was sent a receipt for a transfer from Mexico, and that the money from that transfer never arrived.

She says the tenants then arrived, and later disappeared for weeks.

She says that on a day a locksmith was involved with the apartment door, Valeria reportedly said she would come in the early afternoon, and did not come.

She shared her report to warn others in the Mexican community, so more people would not be left in the same position.

What this page does not show

The shared screenshot did not clearly show the dates or the amount involved, so this page lists neither. One further name fragment in that screenshot was too unclear to read, so it has been left out rather than guessed.
