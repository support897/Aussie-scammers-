The mattress that was never paid for — a seller's story from Mexico

Posted 11 October 2026 · Shared by a seller in Mexico, who stays anonymous in this story

This story was first told in a Facebook post on 29 April 2026, and it was then published in the news, by the Mexican news site La Nigua. It happened in the Riviera Veracruzana, in Veracruz, Mexico. The seller's post is in Spanish — this page is an English summary, and his chat screenshot below is translated into English.

What happened

A woman contacted the seller about a mattress he had for sale. They agreed on a price and on delivery. As a favour, he paid extra freight himself, because she said she could not pick the mattress up.

The delivery went to Lomas de La Rioja, in the Riviera Veracruzana. The seller and the delivery man set the mattress and its base up for her.

When it was time to pay, she said she had no cash and would transfer the money. He gave her his bank details. Minutes passed, and nothing arrived. Her reasons, as he tells them, were: her account was international; his bank was not one of the well-known ones; and the money would land in a few hours.

The delivery man was waiting to be paid for his time, so the seller left, trusting the transfer would come. It never did.

After that, he says, his messages were ignored, his calls were not answered, and in the end she blocked him.

In his post, the seller says more reports about the same woman and her husband have come out since, and he linked to another post about them. The news report about his story also describes the earlier Australian report about a Valeria "H" and her husband Joshua "H" on the Gold Coast — that is Story 1 on this blog.

The chat, in English

In the seller's phone, the buyer's number is saved as "Colchon Hackett" — in English, "Mattress Hackett". The messages in the screenshot say:

  • Seller: "Good afternoon, I hope you are well. I am the person with the mattress. I wanted to check if I could confirm the delivery with you 🚚"
  • Buyer: "Yes, thank you very much." — "3:30, I'll see you there."
  • Seller: "Ok, perfect." — "Shall I see you at La Rioja, or here where I have it stored?"
  • Buyer: "At La Rioja, please." — "What is your name? Because to get in, I have to give them a code."
  • Seller: he gives his name (covered in the screenshot) and sends the entry pass for the delivery: dated 25/03/2026 and marked "Furniture delivery". The address and the QR code on the pass are covered in the screenshot.

The details
  • When: the delivery entry pass is dated 25 March 2026; the seller's post is dated 29 April 2026.
  • Where: Lomas de La Rioja, Riviera Veracruzana, Veracruz, Mexico.
  • In short: a mattress and base, delivered and set up; payment promised by transfer and never received; reasons given about an international account and a bank that was not well known; then messages ignored, calls unanswered, and the seller blocked.
  • Reported loss: the price of the mattress and its base — the amount is not stated in his post, so it is not listed here. The seller also paid extra freight himself.
  • Reported to: he published it in a Facebook group to warn others; the La Nigua news site then reported the story.
