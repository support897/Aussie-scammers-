The rent, the receipt and the locksmith day — an apartment report

Posted 9 October 2026 · Story told by Karla Trujillo

Karla's report was in Spanish, in the screenshots that came with my Facebook post. This is a short English summary of it, in my own words. — Ilse

What happened

She reports Valeria, under the names Valeria Hojem and Valeria Gallardo, over a rented apartment.

She says the agreed rent was not paid. She says she was then blocked, so she could no longer reach them.

At the start, she says, she was sent a receipt for a transfer from Mexico. She says the money from that transfer never arrived, and that the receipt was false.

She says they then disappeared for weeks.

On a day a locksmith was involved with the apartment, she says Valeria told her she would come between 2 and 3 in the afternoon. She did not come.

She shared her report to warn others in the Mexican community, so more people would not be left in the same position.

The details
  • When: Not shared.
  • Where: Not shared.
  • In short: agreed rent on an apartment not paid; a transfer receipt from Mexico that she says was false, with the money never arriving; contact blocked; weeks of disappearance; a promised 2–3pm arrival on a locksmith day that did not happen.
  • Reported to: Not shared.

The screenshot I was given did not clearly show the dates or the amount involved, so neither is listed here.
