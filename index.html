Why these stories are in one place

This blog puts in one place the stories people shared after a Facebook warning post on 30 June 2025. That post was written by a woman on the Gold Coast, Ilse, about work she did for a couple — Valeria Hacket (also written Hackett) and Joshua Hacket — where, she says, her pay stopped, and the pay for work she had already done was refused. She wrote it because she did not want to stay quiet, and because she wanted to warn people in her own community.

After that post, people wrote back with stories of their own. A guest wrote about a stay that went wrong. One person sent a report about rent on an apartment. Months later, in September 2026, a registered breeder shared a story about a puppy and a payment that failed. And in October 2026, another woman shared a short message about contact and questions.

Each story is a personal report — what that person says happened to them. None of it is a court finding.

The stories are here so the next person can read them and check first — before they say yes to work, a stay, a rental, or a puppy. That is the whole reason for this blog.

More stories arrive from time to time. A person checks every story before it is added here. The people who share stories can stay anonymous, and most do.

The stories so far

  • 30 June 2025 — The pay that stopped — Ilse's Airbnb and baby-care work
    Ilse's own story. She managed an Airbnb while she waited for baby-care work to begin, and her pay stopped about a month and a half before she wrote her post.
  • 30 June 2025 — Cancelled hours before arrival — a guest's Airbnb stay
    A guest wrote this under the post. They say the booking was cancelled hours before they arrived, that they cut the stay short, and that they lost money.
  • 30 June 2025 — The rent, the receipt and the locksmith day — an apartment report
    This report was written in Spanish. The person who reported it says the agreed rent was not paid, that they were blocked, and that a transfer receipt they were sent did not match any money arriving.
  • September 2026 — A puppy, a payment that failed — a breeder's story
    A registered breeder of 27 years shared this story, and says it is the first time anything like this has happened. The breeder asked to stay anonymous.
  • October 2026 — Watching, contact and questions — shared by a woman who asked to stay anonymous
    A short message shared on 5 October 2026: Valeria had visited her TikTok profile that day, and she had questions about contact. Her full story has not been shared with this blog yet.

Updates

The rest of this site

The names and profiles page lists the names people used in their reports, because the names and profiles can change. If something like this happened to you, you can share your story — every story is checked by a person first, nothing is posted automatically, and you can stay anonymous. Reports about other people go in a separate section, never mixed in with these. And if a story here is about you, the right of reply page is where you can answer it, ask for a correction, or ask for it to be taken down.

Their social media

Screenshots shared with the reports show a Facebook profile under the name Valeria Hackett. As shown there, the profile lists Gold Coast, Queensland and Macquarie University, and it is shown as locked. A Messenger chat in the same name appears in the screenshots too.

Reports also used the names Valeria Hojem and Valeria Gallardo. Names and profiles can change — the full list is on the names and profiles page.

If you need help, not just a blog

A website is not the only step. You can also report what happened to you:

  • Report a scam to Scamwatch — Australia's national scam reporting service.
  • Report to ReportCyber — the Australian Government cybercrime reporting service.
  • For a local police report, contact your local police station or the Police Assistance Line in your state.

If you are in danger right now, call 000.
