Why I am putting these stories in one place

Hi, I'm Ilse. I live on the Gold Coast, and this little blog is mine.

On 30 June 2025 I wrote a post in a Facebook group about what I say happened to me at work — work I did for a couple here, where my pay stopped, and the pay for work I had already done was refused. I wrote it because I did not want to stay quiet, and because I wanted to warn people in my own community.

After that post, people wrote back. A guest wrote about a stay that went wrong. Somebody sent a report about rent on an apartment. Months later, in September 2026, a dog breeder named Susan wrote to me about a puppy and a payment that failed.

Each of them has their own story. Each story is a personal report — what that person says happened to them, in their own words. None of it is a court finding.

I am putting the stories here, in one place, so the next person can read them and check first — before they say yes to work, a stay, a rental, or a puppy. That is the whole reason for this page.

— Ilse

The rest of this site

The names and profiles page lists the names people used in their reports, because the names and profiles can change. If something like this happened to you, you can share your story — every story is checked by a person first, nothing is posted automatically, and you can stay anonymous. Reports about other people go in a separate section, never mixed in with these. And if a story here is about you, the right of reply page is where you can answer it, ask for a correction, or ask for it to be taken down.

If you need help, not just a blog

A website is not the only step. You can also report what happened to you:

  • Report a scam to Scamwatch — Australia's national scam reporting service.
  • Report to ReportCyber — the Australian Government cybercrime reporting service.
  • For a local police report, contact your local police station or the Police Assistance Line in your state.

If you are in danger right now, call 000.
