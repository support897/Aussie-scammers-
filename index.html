The stories so far

Story 1 — Work that was not paid, as reported

A worker on the Gold Coast says she managed an Airbnb and was promised care work, then says her pay stopped and the work she had already done was not paid.

Story 2 — An Airbnb stay, as reported by a guest

An anonymous guest says a booking was cancelled hours before arrival, the stay was cut short, and money was lost.

Story 3 — Rent on an apartment, as reported

A person says agreed rent was not paid, a transfer receipt did not match money arriving, and contact was then blocked.

Story 4 — A puppy and a payment that failed, as reported

A registered breeder says a puppy was collected, the RightPaw payment later failed, and her calls and messages then went unanswered. She gave permission for her situation to be told.

Get real help

A website is not the only step. You can also report what happened to you here:

  • Report a scam to Scamwatch — Australia's national scam reporting service.
  • Report to ReportCyber — the Australian Government cybercrime reporting service.
  • For a local police report, contact your local police station or the Police Assistance Line in your state.

If you are in danger right now, call 000.
